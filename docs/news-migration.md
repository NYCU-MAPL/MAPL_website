# Legacy news restoration

Reconciled on 2026-11-04 with the live legacy website supplied by the maintainer.

- Recent announcements: https://mapl.cs.nycu.edu.tw/content_old/ (58 visible announcements, January 2024–October 2026).
- Past news and events: the `go_pastnews()` template in https://mapl.cs.nycu.edu.tw/content_old/pages/js/home.js (62 visible announcements, January 2017–November 2023).
- Commented-out HTML was excluded. The stale `go_home()` template was not imported a second time.
- All 120 visible announcements are represented below. Same-month acceptances at the same conference share one news entry, with every paper retained in the details.
- Announcement months are preserved independently of publication/event dates. Legacy spellings `Janurary` and `Feburary` were normalized to ISO months.
- The February 2017 announcement for Harry Chao's January 4 talk keeps its February announcement date and states the actual talk date in the details. The November 2022 APSIPA forum announcement similarly retains its December 1 event date in the details.
- Historic recruitment notices are explicitly archived; only the September 2026 recruitment item retains the featured recruiting animation.
- Existing publication IDs supply canonical paper titles, author names, and paper links. TransTIC's legacy “Human Visualization” wording is normalized to the existing publication's “Human Perception” title. CANFVC is linked to CANF-VC. The source's ISCAS 2023 award recipient “M.-J. Cheng” is retained rather than guessing an expansion.
- “Rate Distortion Learned Transform For Image Compression” (PCS 2025) is not linked to the different DCC paper “Learning Optimal Linear Block Transform by Rate Distortion Minimization.”
- Photo click handlers were converted into ordinary HTTPS photo URLs. Original posters, conference sites, and other resources are retained. Mail addresses remain in recruitment details. Expired meeting credentials are not presented as current access instructions.
- Legacy links are historical references and may no longer be maintained. Tracking parameters were removed; the DIGITIMES Messenger redirect was replaced by its embedded direct article URL.

- Link check: 55 of 57 original legacy-host resource URLs returned HTTP 200. The missing `pages/research/MAPL.pdf` was recovered at `pages/research/MAPL_Intro/MAPL.pdf` (HTTP 200). The missing `PCS_2022.pdf` was replaced with the corresponding existing publication PDF, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2302.06293 (HTTP 200).
